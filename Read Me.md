# Purpose
The purspoe of this repository is to store Your understanding or non-understanding of various concepts, problems and solutions you encounter or produce throughout your learning journey.

# My understanding of notes and note taking
1. A note is a written trace of your understanding or non-understanding of any concepts, problems and solutions you encounter or produce throughout your learning journey.
2. In note taking you try to represent the concept, problem or solution in your own words without or minimally referencing the content. This is essentially the process of active recall and hence the testing effect kicks which causes much of the learning.

# Why use technology at all for note taking?
1. A note is essentially only the trace of your understanding. your understanding can be incorrect many times and must be subject to continuous revision and improvement. so do your notes.
2. Using hand written notes for understanding-storage has many disadvantages. they occupy space and are either very incovinient or impossible to mutate.
3. Using technologies such as markdown, git, github etc. you create the note making system in which notes are mutable, and also have a version history that can be traced back to your first encounter to the the concept.
4. markdown is a text file format and is easy to be version controlled by git as well as it is easier than HTML to make complex text documents.
5. git provides you with commit history, that essentially allows you to see how your understanding evolves. git basically acts as your learning journal here.
6. Github provides you with online backup.
7. Github also provides you with other features such as issues that allow you to effectively divide learning into small tasks and projects that allow you to visualize some of your learning progress.

# Methodology of Learning and note taking
1. Throughly go through the chosen source of conept (video, audio, text)
2. try to recall the concept in your own words.
3. Review the source to check for mistakes you made.
4. Repeat the steps 2 and 3 suffcient enough times.
5. create a markdown file named after the concept at the appropriate location in the repository and recreate your understanding of concepts on that markdown file.
6. perform active recall and review on the concept after 2 days, 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 60 days, 6 moths, 1 year etc. during each retrieval-review session if you feel something is off with your understanding review the original content and make changes to your notes accodingly.
