# Introduction to J1939.
* Clasical CAN 2.0 A and CAN 2.0 B are low level CAN protocols and they only provide a medium of communication not a Language that can be understood by US. This Language is provided by the high level CAN protocols.
* J1939 is one such high level CAN protocol developed by SAE i.e society of automotive engineers.
* Unlike Cars which use OEM defined high level protocols, For heavy duty vehicles J1939 is a common standard across manufacturers.
